New Hope Opportunities serves children, youth, adults and families in an at-risk, low-income community in San Pedro Sula, Honduras, through education, nourishment, personal development, community programs and practical care.

OUR VISION · Education opens doors. Christ-centered care, practical skills and a safe community help people discover opportunity, dignity and hope.

WHY NEW HOPE IS HEREWhere poverty, insecurity and limited resources close doors, New Hope creates a place to learn, belong, grow and encounter the love of God.

01 · WHO WE ARE + WHY WE SERVE

One question
became a community.

In 1998, founder Marne Cottriel came to Honduras on a mission trip. A vision for education and vocational training grew into a permanent community center serving Rivera Hernández, an at-risk, low-income community in San Pedro Sula, Cortés, Honduras.

1998Vision begins after a mission trip2004New Hope becomes a U.S. nonprofit2008Vocational Institute opens2013CHICO reinforcement program begins2016–17El Garage, Hope Kidz and schools expandTODAYEducation, formation, outreach + community
OUR MISSION“Transform lives in the community Rivera Hernandez through Christ centered love and education.”
Education is the open door; mentoring, practical skills, faith and community help students keep moving forward.
